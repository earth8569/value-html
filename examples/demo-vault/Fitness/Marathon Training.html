Marathon Training

#fitness #running

Summary

A 12-week plan with three runs a week, one long run, and a taper. Fuel it well and protect sleep.

Goal

Finish a first marathon in under five hours, injury free. The plan builds on the base in the Running Plan.

Weekly rhythm

Monday rest, Tuesday easy run, Wednesday strength (Strength Basics), Thursday tempo, Friday mobility (Mobility Routine), Saturday rest, Sunday long run.

Fuelling

Carbohydrates the day before, a gel every 40 minutes, and fluids little and often. Everyday eating follows Nutrition Basics and Meal Prep.

Recovery

Sleep is the training. Follow Sleep and Recovery and record niggles in the Injury Log.

Strength and mobility

Two short sessions a week (Strength Basics) and ten minutes of Mobility Routine after long runs.

Race week

Short easy runs, early nights, pack the bag the evening before, and trust the work.

Mindset

Break it into five-kilometre pieces. Habits from Atomic Habits make the early alarm automatic.

After the race

Two easy weeks, a proper meal, and a review in the Weekly Review.
